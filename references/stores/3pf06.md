**槽位标题（成品用，注意：三条格式各不相同，逐字照抄）：**

```
 ⭐Specification:


 ⭐Features
 ⭐️Package included:
```

（即：① ` ⭐Specification:` 行首 1 空格、⭐ 不带变体选择符、有冒号；② ` ⭐Features` 行首 1 空格、⭐ 不带变体选择符、**无冒号**；③ ` ⭐️Package included:` 行首 1 空格、⭐️ 带变体选择符、included 小写、有冒号）

**结尾块（原文照存，行首有一个空格）：**

 💕💕If you have any questions, please leave us a message and we will be happy to serve you. If you are satisfied with our products, I hope you can give us a 5-star review, thank you!
